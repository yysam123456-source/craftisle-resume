Craftisle Resume – Free Open-Source Resume Builder

Craftisle Resume is a free and open-source resume builder that simplifies the process of creating, updating, and sharing your professional resume. Supports PDF export, multiple templates, real-time preview, and AI-powered content suggestions.

Features

  • Free and open-source – no subscription required
  • Multiple professional templates
  • Real-time PDF preview and export
  • AI-powered resume suggestions
  • Privacy-friendly – your data stays with you
  • Supports multiple languages and locales

Get Started

Create your professional resume in minutes. No registration required for basic features. Visit resume.craftisle.com to start building.

Craftisle Resume is open-source on GitHub. Contact: admin@craftisle.com
